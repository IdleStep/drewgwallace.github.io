Home Alert Backup

A small personal tool that keeps an off-site copy of motion snapshots from my home camera system.

Home Alert Backup is built and used by one person, its owner, Drew Wallace. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it is not available for download or sign-up.

What it does

When the cameras at my home detect motion, a scheduled job copies the resulting still images to a folder in my own Google Drive. If the recording server at home were ever lost or stolen, the snapshots would still exist off-site. Old snapshots are deleted from Drive automatically after 30 days.

Google access

The tool asks only for the Google Drive "drive.file" permission. That permission limits it to files and folders the tool itself created. It cannot see or change any other file in the account.
